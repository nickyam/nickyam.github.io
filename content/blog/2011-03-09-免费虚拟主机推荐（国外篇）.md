具体的参数如下：

\* 1200 MB disk space \* 40 GB Monthly transfer \* FTP Account \* File Manager \* POP email accounts \* Webmail \* Website Builder \* Automatic script installer \* 40 Add-on Domains \* 40 MySQL databases \* 99.9% Service uptime

\* 40 Sub Domains \* 40 Parked Domains \* Account Statistics \* Clustered servers \* Password protected folders \* PHP 5.2.12 \* Php MyAdmin \* Vista Control Panel \* Zend Optimizer \* GZIP compression \* Free for EVER
